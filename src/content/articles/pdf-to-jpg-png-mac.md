# How to Export PDF Pages to JPG, PNG, or Single PDFs on Mac

You need one slide or one contract page as an image. A screenshot feels quick, but it hands you blurry text, an awkward crop, and a result that looks improvised. Exporting the page properly keeps it sharp and consistently sized.

Converleon turns PDF pages into clean image files, or splits a document into separate PDFs, in a single pass — all offline.

## Stop screenshotting your PDFs

Screenshots drop resolution and muddy the text. A real page export gives you high-quality JPG, PNG, TIFF, or HEIC output, or single-page PDFs when you just need to break a document apart.

## What you need

- A Mac on macOS 13.5 or later
- Converleon from the App Store
- Your multi-page PDF

## Step by step

Converleon's PDF engine writes one output file per page.

**1. Drop your PDF.** Drag it onto the Converleon icon. Multiple PDFs at once works for batch jobs.

**2. Pick a format.** Choose based on what you're after:
- **Images** — JPG or PNG for the web, HEIC for compact storage, TIFF for print.
- **Documents** — PDF output to split the file into single-page PDFs.

**3. Done.** Processing runs locally. A 10-page source gives you 10 numbered files, in order.

## Why page-by-page beats a web tool

Contracts and reports tend to carry sensitive data, so exporting offline avoids a risky upload — the [same privacy logic](/blog/stop-using-online-converters) that runs through Converleon. The text also stays crisp, with PNG and TIFF available when you want lossless quality for design or print.

## FAQ

**Can I split a PDF into smaller PDFs?**
Yes. Choose PDF as the export format and each page saves as its own PDF.

**Will the images come out blurry?**
No. Pages render at high resolution, so they hold up in presentations and print. PNG and TIFF are the lossless picks.

**Can I export pages from several PDFs at once?**
Yes. Drop them together and export in one batch, mixing outputs like JPG and single-page PDF if you need to.
